# Requesting a Cohort (Informational)

> **Quick answer:** Cohort requests are **submitted by your CMO Project Manager, not by investigators** — and the request process is actively changing. This page explains, at a high level, what a cohort request is and what your PM needs from you, so you can give them the right information. It is **not** a submission how-to.

> ⚠️ **PM-managed and in flux.** The mechanics of submitting a cohort request are handled by the CMO Project Management team and are currently being changed. Do not rely on this page for submission steps. If you want a cohort analyzed, **contact your CMO Project Manager** (skicmopm@mskcc.org).

## What a Cohort Request Is

A cohort request tells the Tempo system which samples to group and analyze together as a cohort, and who should receive the results. Your CMO PM prepares and submits it on your behalf.

## What Your PM Needs From You

To submit a request, your Project Manager generally needs:

- **The samples / pairs** you want analyzed together as a cohort.
- **Who should have access** — every analyst, PI, and investigator who should be able to read the results. This becomes the **`endUsers`** list on the request. **Anyone not on this list will not have access when the cohort is delivered**, so make sure your PM has the complete list up front.
- **Project information** so results are associated with the correct project.

## Adding Access Later

If someone needs access **after** delivery, they can be added — contact your CMO Project Manager to update the access list. See [accessing-results.md](accessing-results.md#who-has-access-endusers).
