# Data Use Policies for WES Repository Results

> **Quick answer:** WES recapture results are an MSKCC institutional resource subject to embargo policies. BAM files and repository data cannot leave the MSK firewall without IRB and institutional approval, dbGaP submissions must be coordinated with CMO PM and CCS, and abstracts/manuscripts must be registered with the Research Data Governance (RDG) Committee at least two weeks before submission.

These policies are stated in the delivery email. They apply to everyone with access to a delivered cohort.

## Institutional Resource & Embargo

Results from Whole Exome Sequencing of recaptured libraries from clinical MSK-IMPACT are considered an **institutional resource** and are made available to all investigators at MSKCC via the shared WES repository. Use of the data is subject to accompanying **embargo policies**. Details on the resource and embargo terms are referenced from the delivery email; if you need the current policy, contact CMO Project Management.

## Firewall / IRB

> **BAM files and data from the WES repository cannot be moved or copied outside the firewall without IRB and institutional approval.**

Keep repository data inside MSK systems unless you have obtained the required approvals.

## dbGaP Submissions

Any submission of BAM files from the WES repository to **dbGaP for a publication must be coordinated** with:

- the **CMO Project Management (CMO PM)** team, and
- the **CMO Computational Science (CCS)** team.

Do not submit independently — reach out before you begin.

## Publication Registration (RDG)

Abstracts and manuscripts involving analysis of MSK-IMPACT WES Recapture data or results must be **registered with the Memorial Hospital Research Data Governance (RDG) Committee at least 2 weeks prior to submission.**
